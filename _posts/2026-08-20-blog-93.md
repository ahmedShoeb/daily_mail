## Key Points

- MagicHour conducted a comparative test between Fable 5 and Sol 5.6 AI models for video creation
- Both models were given identical tasks to create four 15-second videos: three ads and one mini-documentary
- The test focused on evaluating creativity and taste using a structured production pipeline with ten stages
- Fable generally matched briefs more literally while Sol took bolder creative risks with concepts further from original briefs
- Both models struggled with celebrity voice tools, with six of eight runs avoiding them entirely
- Audio synchronization was consistently poor across all videos with sound beats often landing after cuts
- Fable was 80% more expensive on average due to higher token usage compared to Sol
- The article concludes that AI models still lack good taste and cannot replace human judgement in creative work
- Both models were conservative with credits despite having budget remaining for iterations
- The complete test harness is open-source and available for others to replicate
