## Key Points

Google's TPUv7 Ironwood delivers up to 50% better performance per dollar compared to NVIDIA's B200/B300 GPUs in apples-to-apples inference comparisons
TPUv7 costs approximately $0.181 per million total tokens at 100 tokens/s/user interactivity, compared to $0.222 for B200 and $0.276 for B300
The new TorchTPU stack provides native PyTorch support for TPUs, moving away from the previous JAX-based TorchAX translation layer
Google plans to open-source TorchTPU in mid-October 2026, making TPUs a first-class citizen in vLLM and SGLang serving stacks
TPUv7 Ironwood features two separate compute dies per chip with native FP8 support, each running independent logical devices
Major optimizations include DP attention, MoE routing improvements, Gated DeltaNet kernel enhancements, and better communication scheduling
Despite advantages, TPUv7 lacks native FP4 support, giving NVIDIA an edge in FP4 comparisons - but TPUv8i will address this
Google is working on externalizing disaggregated serving, speculative decoding, and KV-cache offloading for long-context agentic workloads
Anthropic has committed to over one million TPUs (400k+ purchased, 600k+ rented through GCP), making them the biggest TPU user surpassing DeepMind
TPU's co-designed system architecture with 3D torus networking and custom ICI fabric enables competitive scaling despite architectural differences from GPUs
