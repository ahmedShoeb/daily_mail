## Key Points

- OpenAI has launched Rosalind Workbench, a central environment designed specifically for life science researchers
- The platform brings together scientific tools, specialized biology models, and data analysis workflows in one virtual environment
- Rosalind Workbench builds on GPT-Rosalind, OpenAI's dedicated life sciences model combining frontier reasoning with specialized tool orchestration
- The platform is available in research preview through the ChatGPT app, allowing researchers to try guided scientific tasks
- Key features include connecting biological questions with evidence, specialized viewers for examining scientific data, and reducing gaps between research steps
- Research mode is available for complex biological questions and advanced workflows, while Explore mode handles general scientific questions
- The platform covers applications across medicinal chemistry, genomics, wet-lab assistance, protein design, small-molecule design, and experimental validation
- Rosalind NGS Workbench specifically helps connect sequencing analysis steps in one reviewable process
- The goal is to accelerate research by keeping questions, tools, analysis, and evidence in one place, reducing time spent on coordination
- Verified organization members can request access to Research mode, with individual access coming soon
