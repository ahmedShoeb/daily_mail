## Key Points

- Z.ai announced GLM-5.3, a Chinese language model with 750B parameters that surpasses competitors like Moonshot AI's Kimi K3 and in some cases Claude Fable 5 or GPT-5.6-Sol on benchmarks
- GLM-5.3 achieves frontier-level performance with only about one-third the parameters of Kimi K3, showing exceptional scaling efficiency
- The model is the same base as GLM-5.2 but with substantially extended post-training, particularly reinforcement learning (RL) environments
- Z.ai has been working on GLM models since 2019, giving them longer experience than most companies in the industry
- Chinese labs keep pace with American counterparts despite resource disadvantages through faster release cycles and benchmark focus
- Contrary to common assumptions, the article argues distillation is not the major factor in their success
- Z.ai releases models in days rather than months, using the time American labs spend on pre-release testing for benchmark hillclimbing
- The Chinese RL data industry is taking off, with Chinese labs buying RL environments from American data companies
- GLM-5.3 shows exceptional capabilities in cybersecurity tasks like vulnerability discovery and exploit analysis
- The model acknowledges dual-use risks and is taking a staged release approach with safety evaluations before full open-weight publication
