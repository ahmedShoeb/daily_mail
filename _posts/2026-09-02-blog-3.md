## Key Points

- Software engineers now need to provide 'value over replacement' compared to AI models that can code for $100/month
- Current AI models are competent coders but make mistakes due to lack of context - not knowing codebase specifics or being overly paranoid
- Engineers can add value through deep familiarity with codebases, catching context-specific errors AI would miss
- Technical communication is another durable skill where humans excel, as AI writing has become unnatural and harder to understand
- Engineers should avoid being 'meat proxies' who just feed requests to AI without adding value
- AI-generated content is increasingly ignored by colleagues who develop 'AI-blindness'
- Communication ability may be independent from intelligence, creating a lasting role for human synthesists
- Using multiple AI agents ('software factory') is still risky as these features will eventually be automated
- The solution is to leverage human expertise to fill gaps that AI can't handle effectively
