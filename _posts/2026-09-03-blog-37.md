## Key Points

- ChatGPT Ads has reached $1 billion in annualized revenue run rate in less than 200 days after launch
- The platform is now used by tens of thousands of advertisers and continues global expansion
- Starting soon, advertisers in India, Europe, Middle East, and North Africa can purchase ads directly via Ads Manager
- ChatGPT Ads uses conversation context to show relevant ads related to what users are exploring in the moment
- Ads are always clearly labeled and separate from ChatGPT's answers, with no influence on AI responses
- The platform protects user privacy - advertisers don't get access to private conversations
- ChatGPT Ads is now available in over 40 countries through OpenAI's solutions team and partners
- Small and medium-sized businesses now represent a significant share of the advertising business
- Recent success stories include an ecommerce advertiser achieving 3x return on ad spend
- OpenAI plans to expand to more markets, introduce new formats, and explore more native business-consumer interactions
