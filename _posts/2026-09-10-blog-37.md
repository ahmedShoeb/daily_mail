## Key Points

- Contrails are thin white streaks formed behind airplanes in cold, humid air, with persistent ones contributing to warming by trapping heat
- Contrails account for roughly one-third of aviation's total climate impact
- Google has partnered with Cathay Pacific for contrail avoidance trials on ultra-long-haul flights in the Asia-Pacific region
- Early trials with over 80 flights achieved roughly 40% estimated reduction in contrail warming impact
- The system uses AI predictions, satellite imagery, and advanced weather intelligence to identify contrail-forming zones
- Pilots receive actionable data through in-flight Wi-Fi and proprietary Electronic Flight Folders without interrupting cockpit workflows
- The Hong Kong-Singapore corridor accounted for more than 50% of trial emissions reductions due to frequent contrail-forming conditions
- Google is scaling the partnership with Cathay Pacific and partnering with nonprofit Contrails.org to advance contrail science
- Contrail mitigation is considered one of the most immediately available, scalable, and cost-effective ways to reduce aviation's climate footprint
- This approach uses existing aircraft and fuel technology and can be implemented now
