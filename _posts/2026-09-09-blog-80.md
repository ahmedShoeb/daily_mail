## Key Points

- US data center capacity is projected to grow from 25 gigawatts to 70 gigawatts over the next five years
- The global AI data center buildout is estimated to cost $5 trillion through 2030
- Data centers are typically financed with 70% or more debt, creating a $4 trillion AI debt wave
- This $4 trillion debt equals 34% of the entire US corporate bond market
- The AI debt triples the outstanding commercial paper market and equals 91% of the US municipal bond market
- Annual AI revenue must reach $1.2 to $1.5 trillion by 2030 to service this debt (from current $100-200 billion)
- Achieving this requires 55% compound annual growth rate over next 5 years
- Today's hyperscalers are growing between 37% (AWS) and 82% (Google Cloud) annually
- The financing of AI infrastructure represents a macroeconomic credit event rivaling the largest debt expansions in financial history
