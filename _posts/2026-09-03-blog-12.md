## Key Points

The European Commission has designated OpenAI's ChatGPT, social media forum Reddit, and gaming platform Roblox as 'very large online platforms' under the EU Digital Services Act.
All three services surpassed 45 million monthly users in the EU, triggering enhanced scrutiny under the DSA.
They now face additional obligations including removing illegal content and protecting the privacy and security of minors.
Failure to comply could result in fines of up to 6 percent of their global revenue.
The designation marks a further expansion of the DSA into generative AI services.
X's AI chatbot Grok is already under investigation under the same law.
The services have until the end of December to comply with the additional obligations.
EU tech chief Henna Virkkunen stated these platforms will be 'held to a higher standard of scrutiny and accountability' given their large impact on society.
