## Key Points

- Mark Zuckerberg outlines Meta's philosophy that superintelligence should empower individuals rather than be centralized in a few institutions
- Meta is focusing on building personal superintelligence agents that work 24/7 on behalf of individuals to improve relationships, health, career, finances, and hobbies
- The company believes invention (not automation) will be AI's greatest contribution, helping people discover new knowledge and create new businesses
- Meta plans to offer free or affordable access to these tools to ensure everyone can benefit, not just the wealthy
- Key safety approach: Rather than restricting AI capabilities, Meta advocates for distributing superintelligence widely to create natural checks and balances
- The company addresses job displacement concerns by predicting that individual capability growth could match or outpace automation
- Meta is implementing independent board governance for model releases and supports open source AI development
- The article outlines specific ways Meta plans to build personal superintelligence, including personalized tutors, creation tools, and business-building capabilities
- Zuckerberg emphasizes that alignment should be about helping people pursue diverse goals rather than enforcing centralized values
- Meta sees American leadership in AI as crucial for democratic values and national security
