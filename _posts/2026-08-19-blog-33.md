## Key Points

- LittleLearner is a research project that creates language models trained on an 88B-token corpus limited to U.S. elementary school curriculum (K-5)
- The models come in three sizes (0.6B, 1.3B, 5B) and each has an unfiltered control model for comparison
- The research explores whether language models can learn capabilities beyond what's explicitly taught in their training data
- Experiments show that scaling, post-training, and in-context learning amplify what the curriculum taught, but don't meaningfully improve out-of-scope performance
- The project provides a controlled sandbox for studying how models acquire knowledge with a clearly defined knowledge boundary
- Researchers are excited about studying whether RL can create new capabilities, observing concepts being learned, and comparing machine vs. child learners
- All model checkpoints are available for academic research purposes
