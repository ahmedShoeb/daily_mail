## Key Points

- Tencent has released Hy4 Preview, a new open weight text-only (no vision) large language model
- Hy4 is significantly larger than its predecessor Hy3, with 770B total parameters (up from 295B) and 49B active parameters (up from 21B)
- The model has a massive 1M token context window, a huge increase from Hy3's 256,000 tokens
- The model weights are 1.56TB on Hugging Face, compared to Hy3's 598GB
- The chat template reveals the model has two reasoning effort levels: 'high' (default) and 'no_think' (reasoning disabled)
- The author tested the model with a 'generate SVG of pelican riding bicycle' prompt and observed interesting reasoning patterns in the trace
- The reasoning trace showed slightly truncated English grammar, suggesting this is more token-efficient for hidden reasoning text
