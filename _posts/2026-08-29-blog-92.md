## Key Points

- Google is reportedly in advanced talks for a deal worth over $1.5 billion to license technology and hire staff from AI coding startup Mechanize
- This would be Google's second major AI coding deal, following a $2.4 billion agreement with Windsurf in July 2025
- The deal would give Google access to Mechanize's technology focused on AI agents that can handle complex software development tasks
- Mechanize was founded in 2025 and recently raised $9.1 million at a $500 million valuation
- The startup aims to enable full automation of the economy starting with software engineering
- AI coding is becoming a key battleground with competition from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Meta all developing similar agentic coding tools
- Google introduced Gemini 3.5 Flash and new agentic coding tools at Google I/O earlier this year
- AI coding agents are shifting from optional assistants to central roles in software development workflows
- Both Google and Mechanize declined to comment, and no agreement has been finalized or announced
- This reflects a broader trend where major tech companies are heavily investing in AI coding technology
