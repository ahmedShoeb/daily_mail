## Key Points

The article discusses Deckard, a Chrome extension that automatically detects AI-generated text content using local models
Deckard runs locally on Macs and communicates with browser extensions via native messaging, requiring about 400MB-1.2GB of RAM
The author compares various open-source AI detection models, with the best performing model (EditLens RoBERTa-large) catching 56.06% of AI text with 2.484% false positives
The creator is motivated by wanting to detect AI content automatically without sending data to third-party services like Pangram
Current local models are significantly worse than commercial services like Pangram, which claims 99.66% detection rate with 0.004% false positives
The developer believes AI detection models will improve over time and looks forward to swapping better models into Deckard as they become available
The article notes that AI text detection is currently an underserved niche, but predicts major social networks will soon scan for AI content
The extension is lightweight enough to run continuously without noticeable impact on battery life or heat generation on MacBook Pro
The author sees value in flagging AI-written content even with imperfect models, as users just need enough suspicion to be aware
The article also mentions we're likely to see more competition in this space as AI detection technology evolves
