## Key Points

/ OpenAI has completed a $7 billion tender offer allowing employees to sell shares back to the company
/ The deal values OpenAI at $852 billion, matching its March 2026 fundraising valuation
/ OpenAI filed confidentially with the SEC in June to prepare for a potential IPO this year
/ The tender offer suggests an IPO may not be happening soon despite previous plans
/ CEO Sam Altman acknowledged the company didn't have its best 12 months recently
/ OpenAI missed internal financial goals in April 2026 according to Wall Street Journal reports
/ The company faces competition from Anthropic, which reportedly became profitable earlier this year
/ OpenAI is shifting strategy to focus on its enterprise business and pare down bets
