## Key Points

- OpenClaw released its largest ever update with over 16,000 pull requests from 933 contributors, including 569 first-time contributors
- The update touches every aspect of OpenClaw: installation, messaging, memory, skills, models, automations, browser and native apps, plugins, and security
- Development took nearly two months instead of the usual rapid release cadence due to accelerated team growth and fundamental rework of OpenClaw's foundation
- Installation is now much simpler, starting with what's already on users' computers (existing ChatGPT/Claude subscriptions, API keys, local models)
- The browser app has been rebuilt as a first-class experience for initial conversations and ongoing work
- OpenClaw now supports shared cloud sessions, turning it into a multiplayer experience for team collaboration
- Users can start with simple workflows (like monitoring school emails) and expand to more complex cross-platform tasks
- The update represents a shift in software relationship - from pre-designed tools to customizable AI assistants users can own and shape
- OpenClaw remains open source, not tied to any single company, model, or AI provider
