## Key Points

- The author's paper 'Tracking Borrows with Regular Expressions' was accepted to OOPSLA '26, describing a new type system for Move using regular expressions to capture heap reachability
- The most labor-intensive part of PL research papers (mechanization of meta-theory and formal proofs) was completed by one person in 4 weeks using Lean and frontier LLMs
- This marks a dramatic shift in programming language research where AI tools now automate the tedious aspects of proof creation and formal verification
- Previously skeptical reactions from the PL community highlighted a publication culture that values visible human effort and struggle
- Research production timelines have accelerated dramatically: what used to take 8-10 person-months can now be done in one month
- POPL submissions nearly doubled from ~350 to 600 in 2026, with most being competent research produced at 10x the usual pace
- Single researchers can now produce what previously required large teams or extensive collaboration networks
- The community will likely raise the bar as AI tools make traditional toy calculus papers trivial to produce
- Future research will need to tackle more ambitious challenges that were unimaginable just a couple of years ago
