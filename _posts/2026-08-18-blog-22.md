## Key Points

- TurboVec is a Rust-based vector index with Python bindings built on Google Research's TurboQuant algorithm
- Achieves 16x compression: a 10 million document corpus that takes 31GB of RAM as float32 fits in just 4GB
- Searches faster than FAISS IndexPQFastScan, averaging 3.4x speedup at 4-bit and 23% faster at 2-bit across different architectures
- Features online ingest with no separate training phase, no parameter tuning, and no rebuilds needed as the corpus grows
- Implements incremental saves with crash-safe persistence - only what changed since last sync is saved
- Supports filter-at-search-time with allowlists that work directly inside the SIMD kernel, avoiding over-fetching
- Uses length-renormalized scoring to correct scalar quantization bias and improve recall, especially at low bit widths
- Includes drop-in replacements for popular ML frameworks: LangChain, LlamaIndex, Haystack, and Agno
- Implements TurboQuant algorithm which normalizes vectors, applies random rotation, uses per-coordinate calibration (TQ+), and performs Lloyd-Max scalar quantization
