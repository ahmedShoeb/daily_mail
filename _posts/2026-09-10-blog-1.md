## Key Points

- Anthropic has signed $517 billion in compute capacity leases over the past 11 months
- The company secured 14.8GW of compute capacity since October last year through various agreements
- Anthropic had previously secured 1GW to 2GW of capacity prior to this expansion period
- Recent major deals include a $45 billion compute capacity agreement with Nscale and a $200 billion agreement with Google for TPUs
- The company has signed data center leases with multiple US developers, including a 191MW agreement with Riot Platforms in Texas
- Anthropic has formed partnerships with cloud providers including Akamai, AWS (including custom Trainium hardware), CoreWeave, and $50 billion with Fluidstack
- Google and AWS will be the largest providers, together contributing 11GW of capacity
- Anthropic previously told investors it expected to spend $180 billion on server rentals through 2029, but this has increased significantly
- The company confidentially filed for an IPO with the SEC in June 2024
