## Key Points

- WikiSkill is a new framework that compiles AI agent experience into persistent knowledge for skill evolution
- It addresses the problem that insights from agent experience remain scattered across optimization histories, limiting systematic reuse
- The framework separates raw execution experience, accumulated knowledge, and executable skills
- WikiSkill continuously consolidates experience into a wiki knowledge base that subsequent skill updates can build on
- It consistently outperforms state-of-the-art skill-evolution methods across diverse benchmarks and models
- Skill evolution complements model scaling: larger models benefit more from evolved skills
- Smaller models with skills can outperform substantially larger models without them
- Evolved skills transfer effectively across models and model families
- Skills evolved by other models can outperform self-evolved skills
- Persistent knowledge accumulation in the wiki is critical for effective skill evolution
