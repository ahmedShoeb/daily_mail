## Key Points

- xAI has released Imagine Image 2.0 as the new Quality Mode on Grok's web-based Imagine service and mobile apps
- The model focuses on creative work requiring controlled layouts, legible typography, and repeatable visual elements
- Image 2.0 can follow detailed instructions, plan typography and layout for dense compositions, and keep small text sharp
- The editing toolkit includes region targeting with magic wand/segmentation, background removal, and combining up to five reference images
- Smart Resize extends images to various frame ratios from 1:2 to 9:16 through square, landscape, and 2:1 formats
- xAI introduces ready-made templates for workflows including photo edits, product shots, headshots, icons, and merchandise
- For video planning, the system can generate characters, locations, and props with consistent visual styles
- According to Arena results, Image 2.0 ranks second worldwide in both text-to-image generation and image editing
- API access is planned but not yet available
- The model appears as SpaceXAI on the Arena benchmarking platform
