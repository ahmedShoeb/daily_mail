## Key Points

- Traditional SDLC processes haven't kept pace with AI's ability to write code rapidly, creating bottlenecks in planning, review, and deployment stages.
- The AI-native SDLC transforms the linear software development process into a loop where AI is embedded at every stage, with automated handovers between phases.
- Key artifacts like intent.md, spec.md, and plan.md become version-controlled documents that both humans and AI can read and act on.
- Humans remain accountable for judgment-based decisions, but their attention shifts to reviewing artifacts flagged by AI rather than starting each stage from scratch.
- The playbook covers six stages: Plan (capturing intent), Design (requirements/design compression), Build (institutional knowledge as files), Test (self-verification), Deploy (AI-assisted review), and Maintain (autonomous operation).
- CLAUDE.md files institutionalize team conventions and knowledge, making them accessible to AI agents at the start of every session.
- Skills encode organizational policies as version-controlled constraints that AI applies during development.
- Hooks serve as guardrails that can block, allow, or require human approval for specific actions.
- Continuous evals in CI ensure AI configuration changes don't degrade performance, creating AI-native QA gates.
- The system supports parallel sessions where engineers can oversee multiple AI-powered development streams simultaneously.
- Governance is maintained through audit trails in git history, with human approval gates preserved for critical decisions.
