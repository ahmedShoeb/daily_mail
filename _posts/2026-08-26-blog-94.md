## Key Points

, The article discusses the evolution of AI agent harnesses, explaining the breakthrough around Christmas 2025 where agents 'started to work' due to models and harnesses improving together
- Harnesses are everything besides model weights that make agents work: tools, context, memory, guardrails - like giving a brain a body
- Harness evolution has three eras: Bolt-On Era (pre-2025), Co-Training Era (present), and the emerging Attention Era (future)
- In the Co-Training Era, models start absorbing harness capabilities into weights - like OpenAI's GPT-5.6 Sol tripling its ARC-AGI-3 score with harness changes alone
- The loop is: train -> absorb -> shed -> repeat, with Anthropic deleting 80% of Claude Code's system prompt as models absorb capabilities
- Future harnesses will become 'attention-interfaces' focusing on human-centric capabilities like permissions, identity, trust, and legibility
- The author predicts within a year, every AI company will ship a human attention policy surface similar to how they ship AGENTS.md now
- The fundamental scarce resource shifts from tokens to synchronous human attention
- The article explains that the Christmas 2025 breakthrough wasn't just model improvement, but the co-evolution of models and harnesses working together
- The ultimate goal is building interfaces to human attention that models will never absorb
