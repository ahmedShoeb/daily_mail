## Key Points

- Salesforce and Anthropic announced 'Claudeforce' - a new way for salespeople to access Salesforce data directly from inside Anthropic's Claude chatbot
- Claudeforce launches as a plugin with 37 pre-built sales skills, allowing Claude users to compose emails, update records, and take other relevant actions
- This marks the first time Salesforce has ever added its 'force' suffix to another company's product name
- Salesforce CEO Marc Benioff called it 'a first in the industry' and predicted this is how all enterprise systems will operate in the future
- Salesforce shares rose 12% in extended trading following the partnership announcement and strong quarterly results
- Anthropic reported explosive growth, with annualized revenue hitting $65 billion at the end of July - a sevenfold increase from a year ago
- The companies are building 'Enterprise Frontier Safeguards' to ensure Salesforce customer data remains private and Anthropic's models 'don't run out of control'
- Claudeforce will launch in preview capacity next month to select pilot customers, with additional skills available later this year
- The partnership comes as Salesforce and software peers face investor concerns about AI tools replacing them
