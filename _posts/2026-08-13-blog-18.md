## Key Points

-## The author joined OpenAI two years ago and needed to build a finance function from the ground up, making AI fundamental to how they work and make decisions.
-## They set two bold ambitions: achieving a 'zero-day close' (real-time, reconciled financial view) and automated, continuously updated forecasting.
-## The article outlines five practical lessons for CFOs: 1) Combine bottom-up experimentation with top-down strategy, 2) Redesign workflows around consequential decisions, 3) Empower finance professionals to build their own AI tools, 4) Maintain human accountability while accelerating work with AI, 5) Measure AI's value through operating performance rather than just usage metrics.
-## The team created IR-GPT, a custom GPT for investor relations that can produce draft answers to diligence questions in seconds, dramatically reducing work that previously took hours.
-## Finance professionals at OpenAI are building custom AI dashboards and tools with ChatGPT Work and Codex, moving from static Excel models to live, interactive tools.
-## Research shows 40% of finance professionals' specialized AI use involves work outside traditional finance, and 22% involves engineering-related tasks.
-## Clear accountability structures are essential - every AI output should connect to reliable sources, and finance should maintain control over approvals and final sign-offs.
-## The vision is for finance to become a real-time function that helps leaders see choices ahead and act sooner while outcomes can still be changed.
-## CFOs have a unique position to lead AI transformation due to their central role in strategy, capital, data, risk, and performance.
