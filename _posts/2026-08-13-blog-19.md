## Key Points

-Mvidia is partnering with six major Wall Street asset managers to create a $500 billion financing platform for AI infrastructure
-The partners include Apollo Global Management, Blackstone, BlackRock, Brookfield Asset Management, Goldman Sachs and KKR
-This marks the first time technology chips are being treated as an investable asset class, similar to commercial real estate or toll roads
-Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang describes AI chips as revenue-generating assets that are productive, long-lived, fungible, and flexible
-The initiative aims to help hyperscalers, AI labs and enterprises build data centers without tapping their own balance sheets
-Wall Street leaders believe AI compute will become a critical asset class driving the next leg of global economic growth
-BlackRock CEO Larry Fink compares this project to the creation of mortgage-backed securities in the 1970s
-Blackstone President Jon Gray says AI demand is outstripping supply, with use at portfolio companies surging sevenfold this year
-This comes after concerns about Big Tech's massive AI investments and their impact on free cash flow and debt levels
-The effort challenges the traditional view of GPUs as rapidly depreciating hardware, transforming them into long-term bankable infrastructure
