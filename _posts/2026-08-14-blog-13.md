## Key Points

- Grok Bot is a new team of always-on AI agents that work 24/7 inside tools and apps like human employees do
- Bots have their own cloud-based computer, sign into existing tools, and work across apps, inboxes, and websites including platforms with no clean API
- They can complete multi-step jobs end-to-end and only return when human approval is needed
- Users message bots like colleagues and they learn workflows, pick up user preferences, and get sharper over time
- At SpaceXAI, bots handle sales CRM updates, marketing campaigns, office operations, bug fixes, and engineering tasks
- Bots can work in parallel, message each other, coordinate in group chats, and pass work between them without human intervention
- They can observe user workflows and save them as routines for future execution, eliminating the need to re-explain processes
- Grok Bot is now available in beta for SuperGrok Heavy, Cursor Ultra, and Cursor Teams Premium subscribers on desktop and iOS
- Early users report it feels more like giving work to capable teammates rather than prompting traditional AI agents
