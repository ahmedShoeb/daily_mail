## Key Points

- Nvidia is shifting its competitive advantage from chip technology dominance to leveraging its massive capital resources as competitors like AMD and Google close the technology gap
- Nvidia recently announced a $500 billion financing partnership with Wall Street firms for its GPUs and is providing up to $105 billion for an OpenAI data center in Ohio
- The company's quarterly free cash flow has grown 18-fold over three years to $48.5 billion, enabling new financial strategies
- Nvidia is using its balance sheet strength to ensure continued AI infrastructure growth despite competition
- The company has been investing heavily in AI ecosystem companies, holding $30.2 billion in marketable equity securities (up from $12.9 billion a year ago)
- CEO Jensen Huang announced GPUs as a new asset class with third-party financing partnerships, with Nvidia able to backstop 25% of every loan
- Despite competition, AI demand remains extremely strong with Anthropic's annualized revenue reaching $65 billion and OpenAI at $40 billion
- Analysts believe Nvidia's financial moves will elongate and make the AI investment cycle more durable
- Nvidia increased its quarterly dividend to 25 cents from a penny and announced an $80 billion stock buyback plan
- The company aims to return roughly 50% of free cash flow to shareholders this year
