## Key Points

- OpenAI is terminating its contract providing models to Cursor (a developer tool) with a proposed shutdown date of November 12, 2026
- This decision comes after SpaceX acquired Cursor, and OpenAI cites concerns about SpaceX's compliance with terms of service
- OpenAI references past violations by Elon Musk's companies: Twitter (now part of SpaceX) broke contract terms, and xAI (also part of SpaceX) violated OpenAI's terms of service
- The custom agreement with Cursor included a limited time window to cancel after a change of control
- OpenAI emphasizes its responsibility to ensure upcoming model 'Astra' is used in accordance with their terms
- The company acknowledges this will affect developers who rely on OpenAI models in Cursor
- OpenAI expresses respect for Cursor's team and product, and commitment to supporting developers through the transition
