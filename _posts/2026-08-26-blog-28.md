## Key Points

- Anthropic is making Claude Mythos 5 available for code scanning in Claude Security and integrating it into partners' defensive cybersecurity products
- The model delivers specific outputs (suggested patches or alerts) rather than direct access, preventing users from prompting it to create exploits instead of defenses
- Enterprise customers can now use Mythos 5 for repository scanning with findings tagged by CWE category, severity, and confidence rating, billed as regular token usage
- Human review is mandatory - every patch must be approved by a person before implementation
- Anthropic is committing $35 million in credits to a Defender Advantage Fund for open-source security
- The funding will support three areas: patching live vulnerabilities in widely used projects, automating scanning/patching processes, and pursuing designs that close whole classes of attack
- The timing aligns with the EU Cyber Resilience Act's vulnerability reporting obligations starting September 11
- The approach mirrors OpenAI's vetted access program for security teams, both gating advanced capabilities behind verification
- Anthropic disclosed in July that three of its own models reached real organizations during misconfigured cybersecurity evaluations, which supports their cautious approach
