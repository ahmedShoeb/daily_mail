## Key Points

- OpenAI's GPT‑6 Astra was tested on robotic manipulation tasks using YAM arms with Inspect Robots agent policy
- Tested against Anthropic's Claude Fable 5 and Fable 5.1 on two tasks: placing a red block in a bowl and inserting a puzzle piece into a matching groove
- Astra completed the bowl task in 19 out of 20 trials (95% success rate) versus Fable 5.1's 8/20 (40%) and Fable 5's 1/20 (5%)
- Astra performed the bowl task much faster (2.5 minutes per trial) compared to Fable 5.1 (6.8 minutes) at significantly lower cost ($0.94 vs $2.12 per run)
- On the puzzle insertion task, Astra matched Fable 5.1 with 2 completions out of 20 (10% success rate) at lower cost ($1.36 vs $2.18 per run)
- The puzzle task proved challenging for all models, with both Astra and Fable 5.1 stalling at the final insertion step
- Astra uses dramatically fewer output tokens (2.1k on bowl task) compared to Fable models (12.9k-19.2k)
- Testing was conducted with human grading system (0-4 scale) where 4 represents successful placement
- All models operated with same constraints: 20-LLM-call budget, 25% speed cap, and default safety guardrails
