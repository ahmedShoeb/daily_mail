## Key Points

- Quantization can make large language models 4x smaller and 2x faster while losing only 5-10% accuracy
- LLMs store billions of parameters as floating point numbers, with most values clustered near zero
- Float sizes can be reduced (from 32-bit to 16-bit, 8-bit, or even 4-bit) while maintaining model functionality
- Symmetric quantization scales values around zero, while asymmetric quantization is more efficient by adapting to data distribution
- Quantization works best in small blocks (32-256 parameters) to contain the impact of outlier values
- Testing shows 16-bit to 8-bit quantization has almost no quality penalty, and 16-bit to 4-bit maintains about 90% performance
- 2-bit quantization appears to be a quality cliff that makes models unusable
- Quantized models are not only smaller but also faster due to reduced data movement within GPUs
- Real-world tests with Qwen3.5 9B show quantized models work well for practical applications
- Tools like llama.cpp provide practical implementations for quantization and benchmarking
