## Key Points

- Anthropic has signed a roughly $45 billion cloud deal with UK-based AI infrastructure company Nscale
- The deal involves Anthropic renting approximately 460 megawatts of compute capacity at an Nscale data center in West Virginia
- Anthropic will use Nvidia's advanced Vera Rubin chips through this facility
- The data center is expected to become operational by the end of 2027
- Anthropic cited growing demand for its Claude AI models causing 'inevitable strain' on infrastructure
- The company has been pursuing multiple infrastructure deals to increase computing capacity
- Anthropic confidentially filed its IPO prospectus with the SEC in June 2026
- The company faces pressure to justify its $965 billion valuation amid competition from OpenAI and Google
- Infrastructure strategy is central to Anthropic's roadmap and Wall Street narrative
