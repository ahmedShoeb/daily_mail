## Key Points

- Artificial Analysis benchmarks LLM intelligence and cost, but their Intelligence vs Cost plot has several limitations
- Key issues include: logarithmic cost scale obscuring price differences, using official API pricing (overpriced for open-weights models), and listing local models at datacenter pricing
- The author created improved plots showing linear cost scale, OpenRouter pricing for open models, and local electricity costs for smaller models
- Top-tier models like Fable 5.1 cost $3.69 per task vs GLM-5.3 at $0.49 (7.5x cheaper), and GLM-5.3-Flash at $0.023 (160x cheaper)
- Chinese models can be as cheap as a mobile phone subscription compared to expensive Anthropic/OpenAI models
- The law of diminishing returns applies: cheaper models are often sufficient for 90% of actual use cases, even for specialized professionals
- Local running costs are calculated based on electricity, with hardware costs becoming significant for models requiring 128GB+ RAM
- For enthusiasts, models like Qwen3.8-27B can run on existing hardware for about $0.015 in electricity per task
