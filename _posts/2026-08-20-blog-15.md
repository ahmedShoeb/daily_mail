## Key Points

- Groq raised $350 million at a $3.5 billion valuation on August 17, 2026
- The valuation is significantly lower than the $6.9 billion valuation in September 2025 before the Nvidia deal
- Nvidia licensed Groq's inference technology and hired much of its senior team in December 2025
- Groq is now positioning itself as an inference-cloud operator that combines its own LPUs with Nvidia systems
- The company operates 13 data centers serving over 6 million developers
- Groq expects to expand from 54 megawatts to more than 200 megawatts of capacity in 2027
- Groq's new business model focuses on building on Nvidia rather than competing against it
- The company plans to use the new capital to serve larger Nvidia-powered clusters for training and inference
- Groq remains private and its financial figures are self-reported rather than independently verified
