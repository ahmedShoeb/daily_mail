## Key Points

- OpenAI has started letting some of its largest enterprise customers pay only when its AI successfully completes tasks, such as end-to-end customer support interactions
- This outcome-based pricing model is limited to select major accounts and hasn't been officially announced by OpenAI
- Under the new arrangement, customers pay only for successful completions rather than per token or per attempt
- The move transfers risk from customers to OpenAI - OpenAI absorbs the cost of failed attempts rather than customers paying for every failed try
- Industry examples show resolution-based pricing around $0.99-$1.50 per successfully resolved customer support conversation
- Outcome-based pricing is becoming a market standard, with 27% of buyers preferring it over consumption-based models (43%) or per-user pricing
- This pricing model helps enterprises forecast costs better and removes a common sales cycle objection
- The challenge is defining success - easy for simple resolutions but harder for complex multi-step agentic tasks
- OpenAI's shift from capacity-based token billing to outcome pricing represents a significant change in their commercial approach
