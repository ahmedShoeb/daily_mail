## Key Points

- Qwen-Drive-1.0 is a vision-language foundation model for autonomous driving built on the Qwen3.5-4B vision-language model
- The model integrates 3D perception, visual question answering, and motion planning within a unified framework
- It includes a BEV (Bird's Eye View) Perception Head for 3D object detection, semantic occupancy prediction, and BEV map segmentation
- A Planning Expert module generates future ego trajectories based on shared VLM representations
- The model uses a staged training strategy combining driving-specific supervision with general-purpose vision-language data
- Achieves strong performance on driving-scene understanding benchmarks while maintaining general vision-language capabilities
- Available for download from Hugging Face and ModelScope with separate modules for VLM, planner-SFT, planner-RL, and perception
- Requires a GPU with 24GB+ memory and supports both direct and reasoning planning modes
- Includes comprehensive evaluation results showing improvements over baseline models on multiple benchmarks
- Released under Apache 2.0 license with full documentation and demo scripts available
