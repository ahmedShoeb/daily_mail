## Key Points

- Google is reportedly collaborating with AMD to develop one of its 10th-generation Tensor Processing Units (TPUs)
- This would mark AMD's first major involvement in a custom AI ASIC project, moving beyond their usual GPU and CPU designs
- The partnership suggests Google may be exploring a new type of hybrid TPU that combines proprietary accelerator technology with on-package CPU cores
- The primary motivation appears to be addressing CPU-heavy workloads, particularly reinforcement learning and agentic model training
- Google's current TPU 8i systems already feature one Google Axion CPU for every two TPUs, showing an increased focus on CPU resources
- AMD's experience with hybrid designs like the Instinct MI300A (which packs both x86 and accelerator chiplets) makes them a logical partner
- Unlike conventional LLM training, reinforcement learning and agentic models require significantly more general-purpose compute alongside accelerator operations
- Integrating CPU cores directly into the TPU package could reduce latency, improve performance, and lower power consumption
- Google has previously worked with Broadcom on TPU design, but AMD's expertise in advanced packaging and SoIC technology would be new to their TPU development
- The collaboration, if accurate, signals a potential shift in AI hardware design toward more CPU-heavy architectures optimized for emerging workloads
