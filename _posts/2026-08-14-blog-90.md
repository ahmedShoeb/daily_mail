## Key Points

WorldTrace is a training-free framework from NVIDIA Research that solves the problem of visual memory collapse in autoregressive video world models when generation exceeds the training horizon
The core issue is that when models generate beyond their training context window, temporal RoPE offsets become out-of-distribution, making cached memories unreadable even though they're physically present
WorldTrace maintains addressable memory by assigning each summary slot a fixed, in-distribution position relative to the current frame, independent of rollout length
The framework has two components: WorldTrace-Field improves coherent long rollouts with rotation-invariant history aggregation, while WorldTrace-Landmark preserves verbatim scene traces for long-range recall
WorldTrace uses a two-tier KV cache system with a verbatim recent window plus summary slots, where keys are aligned into shared canonical phases to avoid destructive phase cancellation
In tests, WorldTrace-Landmark significantly improves episodic recall with Position-Aligned CLIP scores of 0.825 vs 0.627 for sliding-window recall on long ABA paths
WorldTrace-Field shows substantial improvements in temporal coherence, achieving +15.5% TempSSIM over sliding-window at 24x horizon length while also reducing Local Scene Drift
The research was accepted as an oral presentation at ICML 2026 F2S Workshop and received Best Paper award
