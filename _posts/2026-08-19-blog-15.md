## Key Points

- OpenAI exercised vested warrants in July 2026, paying about $100 cash for 10,033,508 Class N shares at $0.00001 each
- This gave OpenAI a 4.22% stake in Cerebras Systems
- The stake had an implied value of roughly $2.3 billion based on Cerebras's Class A trading price of about $229 on August 13
- Weeks later on August174, OpenAI previewed Ultrafast - a service tier running GPT-5.6 Sol on Cerebras hardware at up to 750 output tokens per second
- Ultrafast represents up to 14 times the speed of OpenAI's Standard processing tier
- OpenAI has committed to buy 750 megawatts of inference capacity from Cerebras through 2028, with an option for another 1.25 gigawatts by 2030
- The warrant transaction was tied to a $1 billion secured working-capital loan from Cerebras to OpenAI in January 2026
- OpenAI retains warrants for another 23.4 million shares vesting on capacity, payment, or market-value milestones
- All competitive speed comparisons for the Ultrafast launch were run or characterized by Cerebras itself
- No third-party verification or independent quality evaluation was available for the speed claims at the preview stage
