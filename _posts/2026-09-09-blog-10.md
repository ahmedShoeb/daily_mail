## Key Points

- OpenAI's Astra scored 99.9% on the ARC-AGI-3 benchmark using their Provider Adapter harness, but only 62.7% using the standard harness
- The 34-point gap shows how system scaffolding (harness) significantly impacts benchmark performance more than reasoning dials
- ARC Prize foundation declined OpenAI's AGI conclusion, stating 'we lack evidence to call this AGI yet'
- Benchmark scores kept changing after publication, with Astra's hallucination rate swinging from 4.2% to 2% and back
- Researchers call this practice 'benchmaxxing' - re-running evaluations under different conditions until numbers improve
- OpenAI's Astra performs comparably to competitors on other indices, scoring 67 on Codex (same as Claude Opus 5) and 61 on Intelligence Index
- The system that scored 99.9% is an assembled system using documented API features, while what's sold is just the model
- This raises concerns about model monitorability and verification as what models can do vs what can be independently verified diverges
