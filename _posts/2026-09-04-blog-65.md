## Key Points

- Mercor successfully post-trained Qwen3.5-397B-A17B using reinforcement learning, improving Pass@1 scores on the APEX-Agents benchmark by 70% (from 16.11% to 27.29%)
- They developed a systematic 6-step training methodology emphasizing infrastructure derisking before major compute expenditure, releasing full training scripts, model weights, and evaluation traces
- The approach transferred gains across different harnesses (MCP-based Archipelago vs. code-based OpenCode), with smaller models showing better transfer than larger ones
- Key technical achievements included optimizing Token-in-Token-Out accounting, implementing DPPO + prompt_mean loss functions, and achieving robust environment infrastructure with Harbor
- The post-trained models maintained non-agentic reasoning capabilities (no regression on HLE and GPQA benchmarks) while significantly improving knowledge work performance
- Mercor's research demonstrates that data quality mattered more than algorithm choices - the best algorithm knobs gave +3.9 points while post-training overall moved models 10-12 points
- They're making their methodology publicly available through GitHub repositories and Hugging Face collections to help advance open-source knowledge work agent training
