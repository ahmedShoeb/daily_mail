## Key Points

Random Attention is a new KV-cache eviction policy for reasoning models that keeps prompts and randomly selects generated tokens up to budget limits
The approach is signal-free - it reads no attention scores, value statistics, or calibration data, making eviction rounds extremely fast
In tests on MATH-500, GPQA-Diamond, AIME, HMMT and LiveCodeBench benchmarks, Random Attention matches or beats learned selectors like SnapKV, R-KV, VaSE, and TriAttention
The method was tested on models including Qwen3-4B/14B/32B and Phi-4-reasoning at matched budget levels
Random Attention is reportedly the fastest evictor in both Hugging Face harness and vLLM serving stack implementations
The repository contains the complete eviction engine, evaluation harness, significance tests, efficiency benchmarks, and vLLM port
The paper presents this as a significant breakthrough in efficient KV-cache management for large language model reasoning
The technique maintains performance while dramatically reducing computational overhead compared to existing methods
