## Key Points

- Solar storms can produce intense radiation that raises astronauts' cancer risk and could cause radiation sickness
- Earth's atmosphere and magnetic field protect us, but astronauts heading to the Moon or Mars lack this protection
- No existing spacecraft has enough shielding to stop this radiation effectively
- Startup StemRad proposed shielding astronauts instead of spacecraft with a wearable vest called AstroRad
- The AstroRad vest was tested aboard NASA's uncrewed Artemis I mission to the Moon and back
- The vest performed as well as Orion spacecraft's heavily shielded onboard shelter during simulated solar storm conditions
- Spacecraft shielding faces mass constraints - 'every gram counts' according to StemRad's Oren Milstein
- Traditional approaches required heavy materials like lead that would be impractical for mobility
