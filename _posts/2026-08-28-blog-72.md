## Key Points

In this TechCrunch interview, OpenAI's Head of Product Thibault Sottiaux discusses ChatGPT Work, OpenAI's platform for white-collar AI agents.
ChatGPT Work aims to bring the power of coding agents (previously available only to technical users through Codex) to everyone by packaging it in a safe, delightful way accessible on mobile and web.
Sottiaux leads all core products at OpenAI including API, agent infrastructure, enterprise, ChatGPT classic, ChatGPT Work, and Codex, and reports to Greg Brockman.
ChatGPT Work recently hit 20 million users and is part of the Plus plan at $20/month, which OpenAI sees as offering incredible value for the price.
OpenAI's mission is to 'bring everyone along' with AI technology, transitioning from technical audiences to broader populations as the technology matures.
The company focuses on 'delightful simplicity' in product design, making AI interactions more natural (like ChatGPT Voice) so humans don't have to learn how to use applications.
Sottiaux describes product development as a process of discovery - as they push model capabilities, they discover what the AI is most capable of and build products around those strengths.
OpenAI recently announced major price cuts (80% off with Luna) and aims to make frontier capabilities cheaper over time while including more utility in the same dollar amount.
Regarding privacy concerns about accessing email and iMessages, Sottiaux emphasizes OpenAI's investment in safety stacks and world-class safety alignment.
The interview captures OpenAI's philosophy of making AI technology accessible to the broadest possible population while maintaining safety and delivering increasing value.
