## Key Points

- Within a month of launch, Anthropic's cheaper Opus 5 model overtook its flagship Fable 5 in corporate spending
- Opus 5 costs half of Fable's rates ($5 per million input tokens vs $10 per million for Fable)
- This reversal shows businesses increasingly judge models by cost of acceptable results rather than just capability
- Businesses can easily switch between models with low technical friction, routing routine work to cheaper options
- Ramp's data showed Fable accounted for 6% of Anthropic tokens but 11.4% of spending in July 2026
- Vercel's data conflicted somewhat, showing Fable at 13.2% of gateway spending in July 2026
- Opus 5 matched or beat Fable on bounded coding and office work at lower cost per completed task
- Fable remains intended for long autonomous projects requiring sustained coherence across connected steps
- Anthropic's annualized revenue reached $65 billion in July 2026, up from $47 billion in May
- 43.5% of U.S. businesses pay for Anthropic products vs 39.7% for OpenAI, according to Ramp
