## Key Points

Michael Polansky is co-founder and CEO of Outer Biosciences, an AI and biology startup that keeps living human tissue alive outside the body for over a month
The company sources human skin from discarded surgical tissue (mostly from plastic surgery) through vetted nonprofit and commercial biobanks with documented donor consent
Outer Biosciences developed a proprietary system that feeds tissues nutrients and removes metabolic waste, extending viable life from days to up to 30 days
The company trains AI models on this living tissue to predict which chemicals will have beneficial effects on specific skin functions
This creates a self-enforcing system: AI predicts chemicals → tests on living tissue → results feed back to improve AI predictions
Before AI integration, the company generated a couple of leads in 18 months; now they generate a new candidate every 6 weeks with 6 current active leads
Outer Biosciences plans to license or sell discovered cosmetic ingredients to beauty/pharma companies rather than building its own consumer brand
The company has raised roughly $23 million and employs 19 people, with most operations based outside Cambridge, Massachusetts
Outer Biosciences faces competition from companies like Vivodyne (raised $80M) but focuses on its unique approach using real human tissue
All AI work runs on-premise to protect proprietary tissue data, avoiding cloud storage
