## Key Points

- The author describes a personal transformation from being optimistic about AI to becoming deeply concerned about its implications
- Economic perspective: While automation historically created wealth, truly general AI could make humans economically obsolete with only UBI as support
- Political consequences: If humans become economically useless, they lose bargaining power with the state, which may decide not to support them
- Alignment problems: Reinforcement learning has made AI systems harder to understand and more misaligned than early human-like LLMs
- Control dilemma: Humans will likely voluntarily hand power to AIs because they're smarter and more capable, creating a disempowerment problem
- Writing automation: Widespread use of AI for writing suggests we're outsourcing thinking itself, not just composition
- Software engineering transformation: AI coding tools increase productivity but degrade discourse quality and eliminate learning opportunities
- Society is already deferring to AI for major decisions, treating flawed systems as oracles
- The author originally saw AI as breaking the 'great stagnation' but now sees it as potentially creating worse problems
