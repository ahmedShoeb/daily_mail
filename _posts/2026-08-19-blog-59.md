## Key Points

Andrew Ng presents The AI Engineering Skills Map to help developers prioritize learning and employers hire skilled developers
The skills map is based on analysis of 10,000+ job postings, expert interviews, surveys, and online data synthesis
The four most important AI engineering skills identified are: 1) Building and deploying AI applications, 2) Software engineering fundamentals, 3) Using coding agents, and 4) Shaping the build
Building AI applications requires understanding unpredictable outputs and using statistical techniques to measure, steer, and govern AI systems
Software engineering fundamentals help developers make better tradeoffs in cost, scalability, reliability, and speed when building with AI
Using coding agents effectively requires understanding their limitations, managing context, and orchestrating multiple agents working together
Shaping the build involves having product sense, understanding business context, and participating in deciding what should be built rather than just implementing specs
The distinction is made between AI Engineering skills (broad knowledge all developers need) vs. the AI Engineer role (specific job title)
All developers including full-stack, data, DevOps, and ML engineers will need AI engineering skills
A mindset of continuous learning is essential as AI continues to evolve rapidly
