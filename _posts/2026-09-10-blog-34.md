## Key Points

OpenAI's next DevDay is scheduled for the end of September 2026, with managed agents expected to be a major announcement
The company is working on managed agents similar to what Anthropic currently offers, targeting both businesses and developers
OpenAI has been building its agent ecosystem since custom GPTs in 2023, evolving through agent builders that are now becoming obsolete
A key question for OpenAI is whether it can hit a competitive price point that makes its managed agents more attractive than alternatives
The managed agents will include functionality for creating different agents and environments, managing them, and enabling specific skills and plugins
OpenAI is also preparing demos for business customers, including functionality that would allow ChatGPT ads to resolve directly to agents
These agents could handle conversion funnels, optimizing them far better than traditional advertising solutions
This development could pose a serious threat to major advertisers like Meta and Google until they offer their own similar solutions
DevDay will also feature model updates, security solutions, and other announcements alongside the managed agents
OpenAI's agent ecosystem evolution has happened remarkably quickly, with agent capabilities now covering pipelines automatically without manual connections
