## Key Points

- AI adoption has spread to every function in software teams, with product roles showing the fastest growth from 12% to 34% adoption between January and June 2026
- Executives are personally adopting AI at rates matching or exceeding their teams, with CEOs at large companies (201+ employees) showing the largest jump from 9% to 36% adoption
- AI adoption roughly tripled across companies of all sizes, from startups to enterprises, showing consistent growth regardless of organization size
- Teams are spending more time creating, triaging, and commenting in Linear, with engineering time up roughly 17% on create and triage tasks
- AI now authors nearly half of all issues created in Linear, growing from less than 1 per thousand to just under half of everything created
- Time spent on planning activities (customer requests, docs, and projects) remained steady, suggesting AI has changed execution more than decision-making processes
- New categories of AI-related work emerged including chatting with AI and delegating issues to agents, appearing as a new layer on top of existing work rather than replacing it
- Non-engineers are shipping more code, with product managers attaching pull requests rising from 3% to 10% and designers from675 1% to 8%
- Pull requests opened per workspace increased 111% since June 2024, with most acceleration coming from teams using coding agents
- Teams with coding agents roughly tripled their weekly pull requests from 21 to 65, while teams without only increased from 8 to 10
