## Key Points

Koray Kavukcuoglu, previously DeepMind's CTO and Google's chief AI architect, is taking over as SVP of DeepMind, replacing cofounder Demis Hassabis who becomes chair
He faces intense pressure to close Google's AI performance gap with competitors OpenAI and Anthropic, who have released advanced models while Google hasn't unveiled a frontier model since early 2026
Google has lagged behind particularly in coding capabilities, which analysts say is a "killer use case" where OpenAI and Anthropic are "miles ahead"
Kavukcuoglu's promotion signals Google shifting focus from academic/research projects to improving frontier performance and developer tooling
Google assembled "Code Strike" team to bolster coding capabilities crucial for AGI development
The company delayed Gemini 3.5 Pro release to improve performance in areas like coding, causing Alphabet shares to sink
Kavukcuoglu will oversee Gemini model development, Frontier AI research, and the Gemini app/developer teams, reporting directly to CEO Sundar Pichai
Nearly 90% of Fortune 100 companies use Gemini Enterprise, showing strong commercial success despite technical challenges
Analysts expect faster releases, more experimentation, and an emphasis on product roadmap under Kavukcuoglu's leadership
Google is quietly consolidating AI leadership out of London to Mountain View headquarters, though company remains committed to UK presence
