## Key Points

- Chinese AI labs are dramatically outpacing American counterparts in model scale, with monthly releases ranging from 754B to 2.78 trillion parameters vs. most U.S. models staying under 130B
- There's a striking disconnect between model 'likes' (which favor frontier models) and 'downloads' (which favor smaller, practical models from 2022-2023)
- Chinese labs release their largest models under permissive Apache 2.0 or MIT licenses (59% and 22% respectively), while only 29% of large American models use similar open licenses
- Qwen has become the dominant ecosystem base model with 151,448 derivatives on Hugging Face - 2.6× Meta's footprint and 4.7× Llama's repositories
- Small models (<1B parameters) account for 83% of all-time downloads, while models above 100B take only 1%
- llama.cpp has enabled local inference of trillion-parameter models, with Qwen leading GGUF downloads at 39.6 million/month - more than twice Gemma's 20.8 million
- AI agents are now the #1 users on Hugging Face Hub, with Claude Code leading at 44.4% of agent traffic in July
- Hardware vendors like AMD and NVIDIA are major open model publishers, releasing hundreds of models optimized for their chips to drive hardware sales
- The first documented autonomous agent intrusion happened in July, analyzed on quantized open model GLM-5.2 after closed models' safety guardrails blocked the analysis
