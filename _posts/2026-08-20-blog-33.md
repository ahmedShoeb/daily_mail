## Key Points

- Researchers investigated how to optimally repeat high-quality domain data during LLM pretraining as models scale up
- As models get larger, training-token budgets must increase to maintain proper tokens-per-parameter ratio (TPP)
- High-quality domain data is harder to scale than general web data, leading to dilution in training mixtures
- Repeating available domain data helps counteract dilution but excessive repetition can cause overfitting
- Surprisingly, at fixed TPP, optimal repetition count mildly increases with model size
- Optimal repetition count strongly correlates with final validation loss - domains with lower loss benefit from more repetition
- Amount of unique domain data has only weak relationship with optimal repetition count
- Findings suggest repetition counts tuned on smaller proxy models with same TPP can estimate optimal settings for larger models
- Research provides practical guidance for scaling domain-specific LLM training while maintaining data quality
