## Key Points

- Test-time training allows AI models to continue learning during use rather than being frozen after initial training
- Models can personalize responses like a GPS learning daily traffic patterns rather than giving one-time reroutes
- Memory requirements drop significantly since test-time training uses fixed-size weights instead of growing KV-caches that expand with context length
- Each user gets a slightly different personalized model, requiring providers to maintain separate models per user instead of one shared checkpoint
- Stanford research shows test-time trained models can be up to 2.7 times faster with constant inference latency regardless of context length
- In-Place TTT can boost small 4b models to competitive 128k-context performance without retraining
- Traditional AI is limited by memory while test-time AI is limited by compute and chips - providers choose based on long context vs. many users
- Personalization costs are only worthwhile for applications where personalization creates value, like coding agents learning codebase conventions
- One-off customer support doesn't need personalization and benefits more from shared, frozen models
- Test-time training will be a key part of AI discourse through 2026 and beyond, potentially changing current AI economics
