## Key Points

- Google introduces TimesFM-3, a state-of-the-art time series foundation model with 330 million parameters for multivariate forecasting
- Unlike previous versions limited to univariate forecasting, TimesFM-3 can handle complex multivariate scenarios with multiple coevolving time series
- The model is pre-trained on over 1 trillion time points from real-world and synthetic time-series data
- Uses a novel "lookahead" strategy for past-future covariates, allowing it to incorporate known future signals like promotions or holidays
- Generates entire forecasting horizons in a single forward pass using Contiguous Patch Masking, eliminating iterative loops and reducing error accumulation
- Provides probabilistic forecasting with 9 quantiles (10th to 90th percentile) for each target time series
- Demonstrates practical value in examples like ice cream sales forecasting where it can anticipate 20% sales bumps from planned promotions
- Outperforms competing models on major benchmarks (Gift-Eval, FEV-Bench, and Time) in both point and probabilistic forecasting metrics
- Even in univariate mode, TimesFM-3 matches or outperforms other models before switching to its full multivariate capabilities
- Now available on GitHub and Hugging Face, with BigQuery integration coming soon
