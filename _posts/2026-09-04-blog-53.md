## Key Points

- Google is launching agentic video understanding across their latest Gemini models: Gemini 3.7 Flash, 3.6 Flash, and 3.5 Flash-Lite
- This new capability dramatically reduces token usage by up to 88% and costs by up to 66% while improving accuracy by up to 7% for video analysis
- Unlike static processing that ingests video at fixed frame rates, agentic video understanding allows Gemini models to dynamically search, scan, and inspect target video segments across visual frames, audio, and transcripts
- The feature enables new capabilities like sub-second moment retrieval, long-form 'needle-in-a-haystack' search, more accurate anomaly detection, and precise counting of actions and objects
- Agentic video understanding is available today via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio and the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform
- Google plans to roll out this feature to all users in the Gemini app soon and will use it to power YouTube's 'Ask YouTube' feature on video watch pages in coming months
- Early access partners have seen strong performance improvements while testing the feature
- The capability works by allowing Gemini to take an active, goal-directed role in determining what parts of videos to analyze at what speed and through which modality
- This is especially beneficial for long-form video content (10-minute guides to multi-hour recordings) where static processing previously forced developers to choose between high token costs or losing critical details
- To enable the feature, developers simply set processing to "agentic" in the API configuration
