## Key Points

- OpenAI's AI progress accelerates when the entire system improves together across data centers, chips, frontier models, developer platforms, and products
- Jalapeño, OpenAI's first custom inference chip, outperforms commercial systems in throughput per kilowatt and latency on benchmarks like GPT-OSS 120B
- The chip also performs well on third-party models like DeepSeek R1 and Kimi K2, showing broad compatibility
- Developing chips, software, memory, and network together improves throughput, latency, efficiency, and cost as an integrated system
- OpenAI maintains a diverse compute portfolio including Microsoft, NVIDIA, AWS, AMD, Broadcom, Cerebras, CoreWeave, Oracle, and others
- The strategy focuses on staying on the Pareto frontier for capability, speed, reliability, efficiency, and cost across different workloads
- Project Camellia in Georgia demonstrates OpenAI's approach to data center design focused on customer workloads, job creation, and sustainability
- Better models and optimized systems lead to more useful intelligence per unit of compute, reducing costs for customers
- GPT-5.6 Sol with max reasoning achieved top performance while using 54% fewer output tokens than competitors
- Improved economics create a compounding advantage where efficiency gains fund further research, infrastructure, and safety investments
