## Key Points

- Brad Lightcap, one of OpenAI's longest-serving executives, is leaving the company to 'start something new'
- Lightcap joined OpenAI in 2018 and served four years as chief financial officer before becoming chief operating officer from 2022 until earlier this year
- He was instrumental in building OpenAI's operations and business teams including Finance, Legal, People, CorpSec, GTM/Gov, Partnerships, and more
- In recent months, Lightcap had transitioned to lead special projects at OpenAI during a company shakeup of executive roles
- Lightcap previously worked with CEO Sam Altman at venture capital firm Y Combinator before joining OpenAI
- He described the decision as 'bittersweet' and praised the teams he helped build at OpenAI
- Lightcap hinted at a future venture but didn't provide specific details, saying 'there are a few important new things the world will need to get right' in the AI era
- His departure comes as OpenAI prepares for a major IPO that will have industry-wide significance
- This follows other high-profile departures including Fidji Simo (No. 2 executive), Bill Peebles (former Sora head), and Kevin Weil (VP of Science vertical)
- Lightcap expressed continued belief in OpenAI's mission and excitement to help advance it 'from a different vantage point'
