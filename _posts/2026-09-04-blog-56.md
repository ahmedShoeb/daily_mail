## Key Points

- Achieved 44% accuracy on ARC-AGI-1 benchmark using a small transformer trained from scratch in just 1.5 hours on a 5090 GPU
- The entire training and inference process cost only 67 cents to run, demonstrating remarkable efficiency
- Model performance matches or beats larger LLMs and scores similarly to specialized models like TRM/HRM
- Key improvements included modern architecture upgrades (SwiGlu, RMSnorm), better data diversity, and scaling to 8 layers
- Approach involves training on both train and eval puzzle sets (without label leakage) using supervised learning with only output tokens
- Major cost reductions came from fewer augmentations, switching from AdamW to Normuon optimizer, and using flash attention
- The author argues this shows sample efficiency is achievable and challenges assumptions about needing recursion or massive models
- Open-sourced the code and encourages others to improve on the results without increasing training data
