## Key Points

- ByteDance is developing an AI model for real-time spatial video generation, with founder Zhang Yiming personally overseeing the work
- The model could launch as soon as next month, though timing is not finalized
- It would be built on Seedance (ByteDance's existing video generation system) and create interactive virtual worlds for live streams, dramas, and games
- Targets 20 frames per second with 0.05-second latency, rendered in the cloud rather than on devices
- This cloud-rendering approach reduces computational load on headsets, potentially lowering hardware costs
- The model is reportedly meant to generate worlds responsive to Pico XR headset users' voices and movements
- ByteDance set world models as its top AI priority for 2026, ahead of maintaining Seedance lead and other goals
- The company allocated an eight-figure sum in renminbi for world models - three to four times what rivals spend
- ByteDance is pursuing two routes: vision-language-action for robotics, and 3D simulation for entertainment
- ByteDance recently secured a $30bn loan and has considered up to $70bn in AI capital expenditure
