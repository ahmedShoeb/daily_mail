## Key Points

- DDR5 memory prices have increased by up to 500% in just 12 months, with some kits costing 10x more than their lowest-ever tracked prices
- A 128GB DDR5 kit now costs $3,399, which is 10 times more expensive than the lowest price ever recorded
- Standard 64GB DDR5-5600 kits that cost under $200 in August 2025 now exceed $1,100, representing a 5x price increase
- Even DDR4 memory isn't safe from price spikes, with costs rising 120-180% due to increased demand from builders avoiding expensive DDR5
- European RAM prices have skyrocketed by 345% compared to September 2025, and SSD/hard drive prices have climbed over 125%
- Hyperscale AI datacenter buyers have locked up most of global DRAM production capacity for 2027, making PC and smartphone makers compete for scraps
- Memory chip manufacturers (SK hynix, Samsung, Micron, CXMT) have seen revenues double, triple, or more in just one year
- SK Hynix CEO warns that 2027 will be the worst year for memory supply in industry history, with demand outstripping production well into 2030
- ADATA's Chairman suggests the DRAM crisis could last another 10 years and dismisses the idea of an "AI bubble" bursting soon
- Mainstream DRAM chips are now worth over half as much per kilogram as solid gold due to massive AI datacenter demand
