## Key Points

- The article analyzes six new AI labs ('neolabs') with multi-billion dollar funding competing against OpenAI and Anthropic
- Safe Superintelligence (SSI) is the most credible challenger with $8B raised and earliest forecast for frontier model by January 2029
- Thinking Machines has the largest compute resources but a product-first approach delays their frontier model forecast to December 2030
- Reflection AI is the fastest-hiring lab with $4.6B raised, betting on an open-weight strategy with forecast frontier model by June 2029
- Ineffable Intelligence holds Europe's largest seed at $1.1B and bets against LLMs, forecasting frontier model by June 2035
- Discovery Loop focuses on automating R&D but has the second-lowest compute with frontier model forecast by June 2036
- AMI Labs, led by Yann LeCun, rejects LLM approach and has the smallest resources with frontier model forecast by December 2037
- All neolabs' resources are fractions of OpenAI and Anthropic's scale - best compute is 0.75GW vs OpenAI's 10GW+ Stargate buildout
- The author predicts OpenAI or Anthropic will achieve superintelligence before any neolab reaches serious competitive capabilities
