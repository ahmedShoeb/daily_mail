## Key Points

Meta is preparing to launch its agent super app called Muse, previously known internally as Project Hatch
The product has transitioned from internal testing to a waitlist experience on iOS, though not publicly open yet
Meta's desktop app is being updated with new features like theme support and secondary color selection
A key addition is computer use capability with a new model variant called Ava that supports computer control
Muse would enable autonomous operation of desktop applications and browser control for multi-step tasks
The app aligns with Meta's AI strategy focused on agents that can pursue goals and take action
Previously reported features include using websites, managing schedules, sending emails, and building custom tools
Meta is reportedly considering premium pricing as high as $200 per month for the service
The waitlist approach suggests a controlled rollout rather than a broad public launch
