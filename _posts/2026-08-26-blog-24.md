## Key Points

- Open-source AI models have dramatically increased their market share from 28% to 62% of tokens at Vercel over the last two months, even as OpenAI and Anthropic accelerated in July
- Open-source AI benefits infrastructure demand equally to frontier models since both require similar compute costs per token, though closed frontier tokens will likely capture 60-90% of economic value while representing only 15-25% of total tokens
- Nvidia reportedly can produce 1,000 Vera Rubin racks per day ($630 billion per quarter), with additional CPU and LPU racks plus new revenue-sharing business models with neoclouds
- xAI's Grok-3 is the first model to score over 1400 on Chatbot Arena, outperforming OpenAI and Google's best publicly available reasoning models
- Foundation models without proprietary real-time data and massive distribution for RLHF are becoming rapidly depreciating assets, with Inflection likely being the first of many VC-backed foundation model companies to fail
- Smaller open-source models will dominate vertical applications due to lower inference costs, while only a few massive models with proprietary data and vast distribution will survive
- GPU utilization rate has become the new ROIC for AI companies, with utilization often surprisingly low at many companies but being a key advantage for OpenAI
- Stocks trade short-term on second derivatives of growth metrics and surprises vs expectations, not on YoY growth rates alone
