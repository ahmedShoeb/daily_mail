## Key Points

.### **New Architecture: Full-bandwidth transformer**
- Introduces latent feedback mechanism that widens the vertical feedback channel between decoding steps
- **Key innovation: At each decoding step, the previous top-layer hidden state is fused with the sampled token embedding through a gated linear unit and fed back as the next input**
- Allows non-verbalized computation to re-enter the stack with a renewed depth budget
- Preserves standard transformer architecture, KV cache, and language-modeling objective
- Trained using scheduled multi-pass objective that introduces latent feedback late in pretraining
- Mixes a small fraction of deeper feedback passes for training stability
- Tested on 1B-parameter models trained up to 400B tokens
- Shows improvements in validation loss, 5-shot language-model evaluation, math and coding generation, and instruction-tuned performance
- With negligible per-token decoding overhead, matches or approaches standard transformers trained with roughly 1.5x more tokens
- Produces shorter reasoning traces at equal or better accuracy
