## Key Points

Mistral AI's autonomous AI research system AIRA₃ placed 8th out of ~4,000 teams in a live NVIDIA Kaggle competition, winning Gold by fine-tuning a 30B Nemotron model
AIRA₃ outperformed human competitors who had access to the same frontier tools, demonstrating it can improve AI model capabilities at a level similar to human experts
The system runs many long-running agents in isolated environments that collaborate through a forum for sharing hypotheses and a shared filesystem for solution artifacts
Mistral introduced Muse Glimmer, a 30B-parameter open-weight model optimized for local, always-on agent workflows that runs on consumer hardware like Macs or PCs
Brain2Qwerty v2 is a non-invasive brain-to-text decoder that achieves 61% average word accuracy and 78% accuracy for top participants in real-time sentence decoding
DINOv3 is a state-of-the-art computer vision model trained with self-supervised learning on 1.7B images without labels, producing excellent high-resolution features
The company is releasing various models under permissive licenses including Muse Glimmer (Apache 2.0) and DINOv3 (commercial license) to foster innovation
Mistral demonstrates capability across multiple domains - from GPU kernel optimization to translating ancient Akkadian clay tablets
