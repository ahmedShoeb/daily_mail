## Key Points

- Extropic introduces Z1T, a sparse transformer architecture designed for their probabilistic Z1 chip to improve AI inference energy efficiency
- The Z1 chip uses probabilistic bits (pbits) in a sparsely connected Ising model architecture with ultra-low power consumption
- Z1T shows 14-139x better energy efficiency compared to NVIDIA H100 GPUs depending on GPU utilization rates
- The sparse Z1T architecture is specifically designed for Z1's fixed sparse hardware connectivity of only 16 connections per node
- Extropic combines Z1 with FPGAs in a pipeline-parallel architecture, with FPGAs handling non-Z1-compatible operations like dense matmuls
- Preliminary estimates show Z1T can achieve up to 17,000 tokens/sec with 58.8µs latency per token
- Current implementations show >95% of energy consumption comes from FPGAs, suggesting further efficiency gains possible with chip optimization
- The team discovered that sparse models need about 10x more FLOPs to achieve same loss as dense models, but energy savings of Z1 offset this
- Extropic is open sourcing training recipes and model weights for Z1T to advance AI hardware-algorithm co-design
- This research represents early steps in co-designing transformer architectures specifically for next-gen probabilistic hardware substrates
