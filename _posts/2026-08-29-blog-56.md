## Key Points

NVIDIA reported $96 billion in Q2 revenue, up 106% year-over-year and 18% sequentially, with Q3 guidance of $108 billion ±2%
This marks the first time any semiconductor company will cross $100 billion in quarterly revenue while maintaining triple-digit growth
At $108 billion quarterly run rate, NVIDIA annualizes to $432 billion revenue, making it the world's 6th largest company ahead of Apple, McKesson, and Alphabet
NVIDIA's gross margins of 75% generate $324 billion in annual gross profit, ranking second globally behind only Saudi Aramco
For the first time, 'neoclouds' (AI natives, enterprises, sovereign customers) contributed the majority of net-new Data Center revenue, diversifying NVIDIA's business
Days Sales Outstanding (DSO) jumped from 45 to 60 days, indicating NVIDIA is extending more supplier financing to customers with weaker balance sheets than Big Tech
Revenue growth of 18% sequentially was accompanied by a 64% increase in receivables, signaling changing credit risk with non-hyperscaler customers
CEO Jensen Huang notes a shift from 'one lab driving the buildout' to a 'golden age of new AI labs and startups' scaling in parallel
The business shift raises questions about whether Big Tech's custom silicon (Google TPUs, Amazon Trainium, Meta MTIA) will eventually slow their demand for NVIDIA chips
NVIDIA's growth rate of 106% far outpaces other top global companies (Amazon, Walmart, UnitedHealth) which compound at -3% to 14% annually
