## Key Points

DiG-bench is a new benchmark designed to test scientific discovery capabilities in AI models through text-based games
The benchmark includes 70 interactive games (with 21 publicly released) that require agents to discover unknown rules through experimentation
Each game measures whether an agent can experiment to discover the game's own unknown rules and apply them to solve challenges
All games are human beatable on first attempt, as validated by external human testers
Games come in 7 tiers of increasing difficulty, with even the hardest games being solvable by humans but challenging for current models
The text-based format eliminates visual confounds, testing discovery capability alone in the natural domain of language models
Humans and frontier models play through the same interface with identical game states, action sets, and step budgets
Progress is scored by whether the game can be beaten within a limited number of steps
The benchmark is supported by a community called DisCo where participants can track progress and collaborate
A research paper titled 'DiG-bench: Discovery in Games' was published on arXiv in 2026 (arXiv:2608.12593)
