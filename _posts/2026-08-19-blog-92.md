## Key Points

- Stripe has reportedly finalized a deal to acquire OpenRouter, an AI gateway startup
- The acquisition is valued at over $7 billion according to Bloomberg
- OpenRouter helps customers select different AI models based on their specific needs and budget
- The company previously raised a $113 million Series B at a $1.3 billion valuation in May
- OpenRouter's CEO described the company as 'the equivalent of Stripe for AI' because it provides a single access point for different AI systems and prevents lock-in
- The startup claims to have 8 million global users and provides access to more than 400 AI models
- The Wall Street Journal reported last month that Stripe and OpenRouter were in acquisition talks
- A Stripe spokesperson declined to comment on the rumors or speculation
