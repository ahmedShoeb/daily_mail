## Key Points

- Anthropic's IPO marketing is now expected to begin in mid-October at the earliest
- The listing should be completed days before the U.S. midterm elections in November
- The IPO prospectus public release has been pushed back from early September to late September
- This could potentially be a $2 trillion listing, one of the largest IPOs ever attempted
- Anthropic is working to finalize a $15 billion revolving credit facility as part of the IPO process
- The company is expected to have a tighter window between analyst meetings and prospectus release due to analyst familiarity
- Morgan Stanley, Goldman Sachs, JPMorgan and Citi are among the banks working on the IPO
- The IPO is considered one of the most closely anticipated ever for exposure to the AI industry
- It could come alongside potential listings from other AI companies like OpenAI
- SpaceX went public in June at a record $1.77 trillion valuation, setting a benchmark
