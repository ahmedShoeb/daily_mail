## Key Points

- Nvidia's AI advantage is extending beyond GPUs to data center orchestration systems as AI compute reaches gigawatt scale
- The company is facing increased GPU competition from hyperscalers like Amazon and Google building their own chips
- Nvidia's Vera Rubin architecture includes specialized systems like Vera CPU and Groq 3 LPX accelerator focused on data orchestration
- The Vera CPU shows up to 3x improvement in operations by accelerating data flow and preventing bottlenecks in flash storage
- As AI deployments scale, efficient data orchestration becomes more critical than just raw GPU compute power
- OpenAI's Jalapeño chip uses a similar approach by minimizing data movement within an integrated system
- Nvidia appears to have an early lead in the emerging competition around data center orchestration systems
- The shift represents a new layer of infrastructure competition beyond just processor cycles
